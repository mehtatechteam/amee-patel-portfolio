## ⭐ Client-preferred reference (primary direction)

**`ref-07-giobi-CLIENT-LIKED-THIS.png`** (client comment: *"acha hai ye" — "this one is good"*)

This is a deeper look (WhatsApp gallery view) at the **Giobi** portfolio (option #3 above), showing 3 stacked sections: a giant blocky-wordmark hero over a collage of product/mockup images, a case-study section ("Vibrant Haircare Brand") with clean product photography, and a scrapbook-style "About Me" section with stickers, doodles, and personal photos.

**Why it fits the brief:**
- Light background ✅
- Colorful (via imagery + accent stickers, not saturated UI chrome) ✅
- Reads as professional through generous white space and clean case-study layout, while personality lives in typography + collage details, not gimmicks ✅
- The collage/moodboard format is a natural fit for a designer whose actual work product is *packaging and print mockups* (photographed boxes, brochures) — Amee's asset library (see `client-requirements.md` → Portfolio work to feature) is exactly this kind of photographed-object content, which slots directly into a Giobi-style layout.

## Design direction to build (synthesis, not a copy)

Treat Giobi as the **primary structural/tonal reference** (light, collage-driven, huge personality-forward type), and pull secondary cues from:
- **Theo Wilder** — for the "trusted by / client logos," process timeline, and testimonial-card patterns (a branding/packaging designer's site needs trust signals Giobi's site doesn't show).
- **Qorry'aina** — for resume/skills-chip presentation and the tight editorial grid discipline, useful for a "Services + Expertise" section.
- **Creatiwise** — as a caution: avoid its generic-template flatness; every section must earn a distinct layout rather than reusing one card grid everywhere.

Explicitly avoid: dark-mode agency tropes (Mint & Marble, Dominic) — wrong theme per brief — and any stock "glassmorphism SaaS" look.
